What I took from the references

  • From assistant-ui: almost no colour, thin lines in place of boxed panels, the model picker living inside the message box, and a centred greeting on an empty chat.
  • From Dribbble: a large, confident greeting, and a sidebar that stays quiet so the conversation carries the screen.
  • Ours alone: the free-request gauge at the bottom left. Fifty marks, one spent per request, so someone on a free account always sees what they have left.
